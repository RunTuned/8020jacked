When the app opens

  1. No account and setup not finished: A1, or the setup screen she left. iPhone: the first open from the home screen with no account on this phone goes to A14c (sign in, email pre-filled from the install link), not A1; A1 is the fallback. Android skips A14c (storage shared, already signed in).
  2. 5 or more days unopened: T4 Welcome back first. After it, at most one other interrupt (the first that applies below).
  3. Plan unpaid or ended: S8d first (Today, Train and Food show the locked card T8), not T1. On a new phone: A14 → A14b → A14c → S8d; A12 and A13 are skipped until she has paid, then A12 → A13 → T1. Her data is kept; Progress and data download stay open. Payment didn't go through (S8b): nothing locks; for the 7 retry days the app stays fully open and Today shows one banner (T1b).
  4. A workout in progress, under 24 hours old: W3 resume (W3c in Big screen mode). Older: saved as part-done.
  5. A check-in draft, before the next check-in day: resume at the step she left.
  6. Her 12 weeks just ended: C6.
  7. Otherwise: the last tab used (Today shows T5 if today's workout is done, T7 on a rest or away day).
Offline: a banner on every screen; every tick and set is kept on the phone ("Saved on phone, sends later") and sent when back online.
Never saved: health questions or answers, step counts, or any food detail other than allergies.

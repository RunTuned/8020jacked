F: Food

Meals eaten whenever: Meal 1–4 (2, 3 or 4 a day). Every meal is cheap and easy by default. Any meal swaps for any other. Hand-size portions; grams one tap away. Log a meal with one tap: Ate the plan · Something like it · Something else. Every log has a 5-second Undo. Fasting mode (You → Food): the same food for the day in 2 bigger meals; the daily total never drops. "I buy most food": main meals are 1 ready meal + 1 side; the other meals (breakfast, 4th meal) are grab-and-go items such as "Yoghurt + fruit" or "a sandwich". Each has a size line, no calories. Check-in size changes add or remove a side. A new allergy (S5) takes effect at once: today's unlogged meals are replaced and the new ingredients go on this week's list now.
